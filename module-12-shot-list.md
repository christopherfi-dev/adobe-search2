# Module 12 Shot List: Disputing Distorted Thoughts

**How to use it**

- Copy a phrase from the "Search in transcript" column into Premiere's Text panel search. The playhead jumps to where he says it.
- Drop the cutaway on or just after that phrase (about 5 seconds).
- The search terms work in the Adobe Stock panel in Premiere or in Canva.
- Style used throughout: real people, calm, natural light. No drugs, pills, needles, or graphic scenes.
